about

The Bugs are Toronto's #1 bug-themed dance-pop duo, formed in 2024 by producer Liam Sanagan and underground theatrical-punk performer Nicole Davis (Pizza Bush, My Chemical No Pants, Clown Wedding).

The music ranges from electroclash punk to slick techno pop. Live, Nicole owns the room: confrontational, theatrical, not afraid to get in your face.

Debut EP I AM A BUG out November 2025. LP in the works.

No manager, no agent, just bugs.
